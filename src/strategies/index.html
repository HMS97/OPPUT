Trading Strategies Guide

OPPUT Backtesting Strategies

This guide explains the signal sources and exit strategies available in the OPPUT backtesting engine. Each strategy has unique characteristics suited for different market conditions.

Signal Sources

Signal sources determine when to enter trades. Choose based on your preferred analysis method.

📊

Pattern Detector

Technical Analysis

Uses classic technical indicators to detect bullish/bearish patterns. Best for trend-following and reversal trading.

Indicators Used

  • RSI (14) - Oversold (<30) for CALL, Overbought (>70) for PUT
  • Bollinger Bands (20, 2) - Price touches lower band = CALL, upper band = PUT
  • EMA Crossover (8, 21) - Fast EMA crosses above slow = CALL, below = PUT
  • MACD (12, 26, 9) - Histogram divergence confirms direction

Configuration

ParameterDefaultDescription
Lookback Candles50Number of candles to analyze for patterns
Min Strength30%Minimum signal strength to trigger trade

Best For

Trending markets, swing trading, 1H-4H timeframes

📅

Daily Signal

Multi-Indicator

Combines multiple indicators into a confluence score. Higher scores mean stronger agreement between indicators.

Confluence Factors

  • Trend Direction - EMA stack alignment
  • Momentum - RSI and MACD confirmation
  • Volatility - Bollinger Band position
  • Volume - Volume confirmation of moves

Configuration

ParameterDefaultDescription
Min Confluence Score60Minimum score (0-100) to trigger trade

Best For

Daily timeframe, position trading, high-probability setups

⚡

OI-Scalp (WASP Mean Reversion)

Primary Strategy

Uses Weighted Average Strike Price (WASP) from options open interest data for mean reversion signals. This is the primary strategy capable of 200%+ monthly returns.

How It Works

  • WASP - Calculated from options OI × strike prices (institutional positioning)
  • Mean Reversion - Price deviates from WASP → expect reversion
  • CALL - Price is X% below WASP (buy the dip)
  • PUT - Price is X% above WASP (sell the rip)

📈

OI-Trend

Trend Following

Unlike mean reversion, this trades WITH the trend. When price breaks above/below WASP with momentum, it follows the breakout.

How It Works

  • Breakout Detection - Price crosses WASP with momentum
  • CALL - Price breaks above WASP + momentum up
  • PUT - Price breaks below WASP + momentum down
  • Momentum - Consecutive bars in same direction

Configuration

ParameterDefaultDescription
WASP Period15Smoothing period for WASP
Breakout Threshold %0.3%Minimum deviation to confirm breakout
Momentum Bars3Consecutive bars needed to confirm momentum

Best For

Trending markets, 1H-4H timeframes, larger moves

🔍

OI-Multi-TF (Multi-Timeframe)

Advanced

Uses higher timeframe (4H) for directional bias and lower timeframe (5m) for precise entries. Only takes trades aligned with the trend.

How It Works

  • Bias (4H) - Determines if we're bullish or bearish
  • Entry (5m) - Mean reversion signals on lower TF
  • Filter - Only take 5m CALL if 4H is bullish
  • Filter - Only take 5m PUT if 4H is bearish

Configuration

ParameterDefaultDescription
Entry Deviation %0.2%Deviation for entry signals
Bias Threshold %0.1%Minimum deviation to determine bias

Best For

Filtered high-probability entries, reduced noise, swing trading

📉

IV Signal

Volatility

Uses Implied Volatility data to time entries. Buy when IV is low (cheap options), sell when IV is high (expensive options).

Strategies

  • IV Percentile - Compare current IV to historical range
  • IV Skew - Put/Call IV ratio indicates directional bias
  • IV vs HV - Compare implied to historical volatility

Configuration

ParameterDefaultDescription
StrategyPercentileWhich IV metric to use
Lookback Days20Historical period for comparison

Data Source

DoltHub options database (2019-present)

Best For

Volatility trading, options premium selling, daily timeframe

Exit Strategies

Exit strategies determine when to close trades. Choose based on your risk tolerance and market conditions.

⏱️

Fixed Holding Period

Simple

Exit after a fixed number of bars regardless of profit/loss. Simple and removes emotion from exits.

Configuration

ParameterDefaultDescription
Hold for N bars10Number of candles to hold before exit

Pros

  • Simple, no parameters to optimize
  • Removes emotional decision-making
  • Good for testing signal quality

Cons

  • May exit too early (missing profits)
  • May exit too late (giving back gains)
🔄

Opposite Signal

Adaptive

Exit when the signal source generates an opposite signal. Lets the market tell you when to exit.

Configuration

ParameterDefaultDescription
Min Opposite Strength50%Minimum strength of opposite signal to trigger exit

Pros

  • Adapts to market conditions
  • Can capture extended moves
  • Uses same logic as entries

Cons

  • May stay in losing trades too long
  • Depends on signal source quality
🎯

Butterfly Spread (8:1 R:R)

Advanced

Simulates butterfly spread options payoff. Maximum profit if price ends at target (WASP), maximum loss outside wings.

How It Works

  • Max Profit (8x) - Price ends exactly at target strike
  • Breakeven - Price within profit zone width
  • Max Loss (-1x) - Price outside wings at expiry
  • Time Decay - Simulated theta decay over holding period

Configuration

ParameterDefaultDescription
Profit Zone Width %0.4%Width of profit zone around target

Best For

High-conviction WASP targets, low volatility periods, defined risk
